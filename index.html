Overview

Turning physical measurements into intelligent air-quality insights.

IRIS is a vision-driven indoor air-quality supervision system that integrates camera-based measurement, environmental sensors, physics-based modeling and AI analytics within a unified Digital Twin pipeline.

By connecting heterogeneous measurement sources, IRIS aims to provide more reliable monitoring, early anomaly awareness, short-term forecasting and actionable decision support.

Why IRIS?

More than monitoring. A smarter way to understand indoor air quality.

Traditional monitoring tells you what is happening now. IRIS goes further by connecting different measurement sources, checking their consistency, anticipating changes, and turning data into actionable insights.

01Connect

Connect what already exists

Vision-based Data Acquisition

Bring readings from existing gauges and environmental sensors into one connected data flow without requiring every device to be replaced.

02Understand

See beyond a single measurement

Multi-source Validation

Compare camera readings, sensor data, and model estimates to gain a more reliable view of the monitored environment.

03Anticipate

Anticipate what happens next

Forecasting & Anomaly Detection

Identify unusual conditions and understand how indoor air quality may change over time through forecasting and anomaly detection.

04Act

Turn insight into action

Digital Twin Monitoring & Decision Support

Transform measurements and model insights into clear trends, alerts, and recommendations that help users decide when attention or ventilation action may be needed.

Who IRIS Is For?

Built for the people who monitor, maintain and manage indoor environments.

IRIS is designed for different indoor environments and adapts its information to the needs of the people responsible for monitoring conditions, maintaining measurement systems, and making operational decisions.

01Monitor

Environment Operators

Stay aware of what is happening now.

Monitor indoor air-quality conditions, identify active alerts, review short-term trends, and understand when an area may require attention.

02Maintain

Technical & Maintenance Teams

Keep measurements and devices reliable.

Review sensor and camera status, identify inconsistent readings, inspect measurement history, and detect possible device or calibration issues.

03Manage

Facility Managers & Decision Makers

See the bigger picture.

Review long-term trends, recurring alerts, and area-level risks to support operational planning and improvements to ventilation or indoor environmental conditions.

How It Works?

From physical observation to actionable information

IRIS follows a continuous data flow in which measurements are captured, digitized, synchronized, analyzed, and presented to users.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Cameras and electronic sensors observe the physical environment and its measuring instruments.

  2. 02

    Digitize

    Computer vision converts visual gauge readings into structured measurements with timestamps and confidence information.

  3. 03

    Synchronize

    Measurements update the virtual representation of the monitored environment.

  4. 04

    Analyze

    Physics-based modeling and AI services perform simulation, cross-validation, forecasting, and anomaly detection.

  5. 05

    Support

    The dashboard presents current status, alerts, trends, and recommended operational responses.

Physical data → Digital state → Intelligent analysis → Human decision
